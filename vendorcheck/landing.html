What it catches

The mistakes that cost a payables team money, stopped before payment.

01 · Identity

Look-alike vendors

Resolved by domain and GSTIN, never by name. Three companies called "Shadowfax" return at equal confidence, so a name alone is never enough.

shadowfaxx.in → held
02 · Money

Duplicates and diversion

INV/2026/0451 and INV-2026-451 are the same invoice. A new bank account on an invoice is held, with a call-back drafted to the number on file.

pay once, by key

Directors, namesakes cleared

Built to be audited

It never guesses, and it never moves money on its own.

  • A person approves every payment, and their name is recorded with it.
  • Every fact links to the page or record that states it, with its date.
  • "Cannot verify" when no source settles a question, naming what is missing.
  • Agents never contact anyone. Messages are drafts for a person to send.
